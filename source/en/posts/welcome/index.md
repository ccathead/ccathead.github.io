Welcome to **Catie’s Library**.

This site is both a public technical library and a record in progress. It will collect technical practice, public research and engineering projects, learning notes, and occasional fragments of everyday life.

## What belongs here

- **Technology and research** — reproducible work, traceable sources, and results suitable for public discussion.
- **Learning notes** — organized paths through knowledge rather than unverified fragments.
- **Life** — small and honest observations, with a deliberate private boundary.

Chinese is the primary language of this site. Important pieces will receive an English edition; unfinished translations will be marked clearly.

> 长安.
